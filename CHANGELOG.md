# Changelog

The main changes of every ValarisAI release. Full release notes, downloads and SHA-256 checksums are on the
[Releases](https://github.com/ValarisAI/valarisai-releases/releases) page.

## 1.7.0 — 4 November 2026
- **One window only:** starting ValarisAI again brings the open window to the front instead of opening a second copy.
- A calmer «answer is coming» sign: three small purple dots instead of a blinking block.
- The site opens in the language you chose in the chat; new installs go to `C:\Program Files (x86)\ValarisAI`.
- An update never cuts an answer in half (from 1.6.9).

## 1.6.8 — 4 November 2026
- **The program is now called ValarisAI** (window, tray, installer, shortcuts); the site is valarisai.app. Updates come from the new address, the old one is the second try.
- A short usage agreement on the first start; a safety rule in every chat (no help to break into or spy on others).
- Everything from 1.6.7 below (safer agent, Pro opens folders at once, ✕ buttons).

## 1.6.7 — 1 November 2026
- **Safer agent:** closed a way around the list of allowed programs; it asks before running downloads and code given on the command line, and shows a new file when it creates one. Agent mode asks for consent again.
- **Pro works at once:** folders and the agent open right after activation, without a restart.
- Interface: ✕ buttons in the settings, connectors, tools, voice, «Report a problem» and folder consent windows and in notices; a wider model window with a full-size search field.
- Fixes: calculator tool, a revoked code no longer gives a new one.

## 1.6.6 — 31 October 2026
- **Arabic** is the 10th language (right-to-left): the app, the website, the bots and the documents.
- **Pay for Pro on the website** (valarisai.app/pay): Telegram Stars or crypto (GRAM, USDT on TON, TRON, BSC); the code
  appears on the page with an «Open in the Windows app» button. One purchase works on 2 devices.
- **«Pro ends soon»** notice 7 days before the end; the app refreshes its lists every half hour.
- Interface: new «PRO» plate, the Pro window shows only available features, prices in dollars and Stars.

## 1.6.4 — 31 October 2026
- **Four reasoning levels** on the brain button (off, light, normal, deep) and a level per skill — pay less for simple
  questions, think harder on difficult ones.
- **«Continue» button** under an answer that was cut short.
- **Agent recipes** in Valaris Hub: video from pictures and a voice-over, landing page with a check; ffmpeg for the agent.
- A skill follows you into a new chat; Hub shows new items and the Top 10 first.
- Website: calculator «5 subscriptions or pay per request», skill visit counters without cookies.

## 1.6.3 — 29 October 2026
- «All skills on valarisai.app» from the app opens the Hub with «To the Windows app» first.
- Website: every Hub skill has its own page with an example request and answer.
- Website: every page in 9 languages at its own address (valarisai.app/ru/, /es/, /de/ …).

## 1.6.2 — 29 October 2026
- **Valaris Hub in the app.** A new «Valaris Hub» tab in the «✨ Skill» window: 30 ready-made skills made and
  checked by our team (resumes, business plans, SQL, homework, stories, trips and more), in 9 languages. Without
  Pro you can keep up to 3 of them; Valaris Pro gives all. «Add to Valaris» on
  [valarisai.app/hub](https://valarisai.app/hub) opens the web version on that skill, «in the Windows app» opens the
  installed Valaris (a `valaris://` link; an already open window shows the skill).
- Links to the website pages from the Windows app (Terms, Privacy, Valaris Hub) open valarisai.app.

## 1.6.1 — 28 October 2026
- **AI voice:** answers can be read aloud by a soft neural voice, six voices to choose from.
- **Agent keys:** keyboard shortcuts for agent mode, listed in the in-app help.
- **Task price:** after an agent task Valaris shows what it cost on OpenRouter.
- Website: «Made with Valaris» portfolio of projects built by the agent, with time, price and the task.

## 1.6.0 — 25 October 2026
- **Add to an answer while it is being written:** type while the model answers and press Enter.
- **Hide to the tray:** ✕ can hide Valaris next to the clock instead of closing it.
- OpenRouter shows Valaris correctly in your activity; the installer is checked file by file during the build.
- Website: new [Security and privacy](https://valarisai.app/security.html) page.
