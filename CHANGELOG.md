# Changelog

The main changes of every ValarisAI release. Full release notes, downloads and SHA-256 checksums are on the
[Releases](https://github.com/ValarisAI/valarisai-releases/releases) page.

## 1.6.4 — 31 October 2026
- **Four reasoning levels** on the brain button (off, light, normal, deep) and a level per skill — pay less for simple
  questions, think harder on difficult ones.
- **«Continue» button** under an answer that was cut short.
- **Agent recipes** in ValarisAI Hub: video from pictures and a voice-over, landing page with a check; ffmpeg for the agent.
- A skill follows you into a new chat; Hub shows new items and the Top 10 first.
- Website: calculator «5 subscriptions or pay per request», skill visit counters without cookies.

## 1.6.3 — 29 October 2026
- «All skills on valarisai.app» from the app opens the Hub with «To the Windows app» first.
- Website: every Hub skill has its own page with an example request and answer.
- Website: every page in 9 languages at its own address (valarisai.app/ru/, /es/, /de/ …).

## 1.6.2 — 29 October 2026
- **ValarisAI Hub in the app.** A new «ValarisAI Hub» tab in the «✨ Skill» window: 30 ready-made skills made and
  checked by our team (resumes, business plans, SQL, homework, stories, trips and more), in 9 languages. Without
  Pro you can keep up to 3 of them; ValarisAI Pro gives all. «Add to ValarisAI» on
  [valarisai.app/hub](https://valarisai.app/hub) opens the web version on that skill, «in the Windows app» opens the
  installed ValarisAI (a `valaris://` link; an already open window shows the skill).
- Links to the website pages from the Windows app (Terms, Privacy, ValarisAI Hub) open valarisai.app.

## 1.6.1 — 28 October 2026
- **AI voice:** answers can be read aloud by a soft neural voice, six voices to choose from.
- **Agent keys:** keyboard shortcuts for agent mode, listed in the in-app help.
- **Task price:** after an agent task ValarisAI shows what it cost on OpenRouter.
- Website: «Made with ValarisAI» portfolio of projects built by the agent, with time, price and the task.

## 1.6.0 — 25 October 2026
- **Add to an answer while it is being written:** type while the model answers and press Enter.
- **Hide to the tray:** ✕ can hide ValarisAI next to the clock instead of closing it.
- OpenRouter shows ValarisAI correctly in your activity; the installer is checked file by file during the build.
- Website: new [Security and privacy](https://valarisai.app/security.html) page.
